1. Use the Recovery Utility when the app cannot be installed Normally, you would recover your Vault by installing Vault12 Guard on a new device and requesting assistance from your Guardians through the app. If the app can no longer be downloaded or installed, the open-source Vault12 Recovery Utility ( https://github.com/vault12/recovery-utility ) provides an alternative recovery method.
